About Our School

Mahatma Gandhi Government English Medium School

School Overview

Mahatma Gandhi Government English Medium School, Lalchandpura (Jaipur) is a government school operating under the Mahatma Gandhi English Medium Schools (MGGS) scheme of the Rajasthan Education Department.

We provide quality English medium education from Class 1 to Class 12. Our aim is to give equal educational opportunities to children from all sections of society and prepare them for a bright future.

Address: Chak Bawadi, Mansa Rampura, Lalchandpura, Jaipur - 302012, Rajasthan.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality English medium education, build strong character, and develop academic excellence in every student.

Our Vision

To become a model government English medium school that nurtures confident, responsible and future-ready citizens.
